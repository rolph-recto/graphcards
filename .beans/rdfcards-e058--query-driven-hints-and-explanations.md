Support optional SPARQL bindings for a hint shown before reveal and an explanation shown after reveal, for both basic and multiple-choice presentations. Group duplicate rows consistently, keep hints out of card identity and scheduling, and surface malformed or conflicting values as user-facing presentation errors. Open decisions: exact variable names and cardinality, plain text versus RDF markup, and whether explanations belong in review history.
